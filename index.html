About Task Master

Task Master is a productivity tool based on the Pomodoro technique.

It helps users manage time, stay focused, and complete daily tasks efficiently.

You can organize tasks by priority, track your progress, and enjoy a clear workflow.

Designed to be simple, effective, and easy to use for everyone.

Productivity Tips

  • Break big tasks into smaller steps.
  • Use the Pomodoro timer to stay focused for 25 minutes.
  • Take short breaks to refresh your mind.
  • Review your progress at the end of the day.
  • Celebrate small wins to stay motivated.
